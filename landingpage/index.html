Turn your phone or iPad into a touchpad for your Mac. For a true theater mode experience.

✨ Designed for Effortless Couch and Bed Control

🎬

True Theater Mode

Control YouTube, Netflix, Spotify, and system volume from anywhere in the room. Includes dedicated YouTube deck with PiP, Speed controls, and seek jumps.

🖲️

Single-Handed Thumb Scroll

Slide your thumb along the dedicated right-edge scroll wheel with mechanical haptic ticks to glide through web pages without using two fingers.

⚡

Zero-Lag Gliding Tracking

Sub-pixel micro-interpolation and instant sensitivity controls (0.8x to 4.0x) eliminate pointer jumps for liquid smooth cursor response.

⌨️

Floating Terminal Toolbar

Access Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Opt, Cmd, and arrow keys directly above your software keyboard with modifier latching for effortless shell control.

📱

Zero Mobile Installs

No App Store downloads needed. Open TouchMyMac on your Mac, scan the menu bar QR code with your phone camera, and start controlling immediately.

🎧

Headphone Audio Streaming

Stream Mac audio directly to your phone headphones with a 1-tap Mac speaker mute for private late-night movie watching.

💌

Experimental Audio Streaming Note

Wireless audio routing via ScreenCaptureKit is currently in active experimental stage. While functional for movie watching with Mac speakers muted, we are actively refining buffer latency and compression.
